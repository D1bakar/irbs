Where is my train?

One number tells last stop and next stop.

1. What to type. 5-digit train number works best, like 12301. Train name also works in this demo.

2. How to read it. On time or late by minutes. Last station crossed. Next station with expected time.

3. Refresh. Late trains change fast. Check again every 15–20 minutes. Keep phone charged and some mobile data.

4. If late at night. Wait in the waiting hall, not on empty platforms. Keep food and water. Call 139 if alone or unsure.
